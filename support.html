Getting Started

1

Create a Telegram Bot

Open Telegram and search for @BotFather. Send /newbot and follow the instructions. Save the API token provided.

2

Prepare Your Code

Write your bot in Node.js or Python. Include package.json or requirements.txt. Zip all files.

3

Get Coins

Purchase from the Coin Store or claim your daily free coin. You need coins to deploy bots.

4

Deploy Your Bot

Go to Deploy, upload your code, enter your bot token, and click Deploy. Your bot goes live in seconds!

5

Monitor & Manage

Use Dashboard to start/stop bots, view logs, and Analytics for performance insights.
